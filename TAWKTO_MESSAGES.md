# Tawk.to Custom Messages for Montebay

## Recommended Messages

### Welcome Message
**Primary Option (Recommended):**
```
👋 Hi! I'm here to help you find the right Montebay solution. What's your biggest technical challenge?
```

**Alternative (More Concise):**
```
Hi! How can we help you today?
```

**Alternative (Service-Focused):**
```
Hi! I can help you learn about our AI capabilities, cloud architecture services, or schedule a consultation. What interests you most?
```

---

Choose 2-4 of these based on your most common inquiries:

**Option Set 1 (Service-Focused):**
1. "I have a question about your services"
2. "Tell me about AI capabilities"

### Offline Message

**Standard:**
```
We're currently offline. Leave a message and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
```

**More Personalized:**
```
Thanks for reaching out! We're currently offline, but we'll respond to your message within 24 hours. For urgent matters, email us at contact@montebay.io
```

**With Business Hours:**
```
We're currently offline. Our team typically responds within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, email contact@montebay.io
```

## Brand Voice Guidelines

When customizing messages, keep in mind Montebay's brand voice:

- **Professional but approachable** - Not overly formal, but clear and confident
- **Solution-focused** - Emphasize solving problems, not selling
- **Clear and direct** - Avoid jargon, be straightforward
- **Helpful** - Focus on helping visitors find what they need

---

## Implementation Steps

1. **In Tawk.to Dashboard:**
   - Go to **Administration** → **Chat Widget** → **Chat Widget Settings**
   - Find "Welcome Message" section
   - Enter your chosen welcome message
   - Find "Suggested message" section
   - Add 2-4 suggested messages (one per field)
   - Find "Offline Message" section
   - Enter your offline message

2. **Test:**
   - Use the preview panel on the right
   - Test on your live site after deployment
   - Adjust based on actual visitor questions

3. **Iterate:**
   - Monitor common questions
   - Update suggested messages based on what visitors actually ask
   - Refine welcome message if needed

---

## Pro Tips

- **Keep it short** - Welcome messages should be 1-2 sentences max
- **Be specific** - Suggested messages work best when they're specific actions
- **Match your services** - Suggested messages should align with your main services
- **Test different options** - Try different messages and see which get more engagement
- **Update seasonally** - Adjust messages if you're promoting specific services or offers
