Who We Are

Solving Complex Problems with Clear Systems

Montebay Innovations is a consulting and technology studio focused on solving complex problems with clear, practical systems.

Founded by a senior systems and cloud architect, Montebay brings hands-on experience across cloud infrastructure, automation, product operations, and applied AI. The work is grounded in execution — not theory — shaped by years of building, operating, and fixing systems that businesses depend on every day.

How We Work

Clients come to Montebay when technology feels fragmented, overly complex, or unreliable. The role is to step in, bring clarity, and help design a path forward — whether that means advisory guidance, hands-on implementation, or both.

Montebay operates differently from traditional agencies or consulting firms. Engagements are focused, outcome-driven, and built around trust. The goal is not to sell tools or create dependency, but to help organizations make better decisions and build systems that quietly do their job.

At its core, Montebay Innovations exists to:

🎯

Reduce Complexity

Simplify systems and processes to make technology work for you, not against you.

⚙️

Improve Reliability

Build systems that operate consistently and dependably, day after day.

⏱️

Save Time & Mental Bandwidth

Free up your team to focus on what matters most by automating the routine.

🚀

Turn Strategy into Working Systems

Transform ideas and plans into real, functioning solutions that deliver results.

Whether advising on cloud architecture, streamlining operations through automation, or helping shape a new product from idea to execution, Montebay focuses on practical solutions that last.

Strategic Cyber Risk Advisory

Clarity on cyber and privacy risk — without tools, testing, or disruption.

Organizations today rely on dozens of digital platforms, vendors, and data flows. Yet most leadership teams lack a clear, prioritized understanding of where meaningful cyber and privacy risk actually exists.

Montebay's Strategic Cyber Risk Advisory is a remote, judgment-led assessment designed to help decision-makers understand exposure, tradeoffs, and priorities before committing to tools, audits, or implementation work.

This is not a penetration test.
This is not a compliance certification.
It's an independent, executive-level review focused on what matters most and what to do first.

What This Advisory Delivers

A clear, written assessment that answers:

  • Where meaningful cyber and privacy risk exists today
  • Which risks matter most to the business (and which don't)
  • Where assumptions may be creating blind spots
  • What leadership should prioritize over the next 30–90 days

All delivered asynchronously, without meetings or operational disruption.

How It Works

  1. Structured Intake — You provide limited, targeted inputs and documentation relevant to your environment.
  2. Independent Review — Montebay evaluates risk across access, data handling, vendor dependency, and operational exposure.
  3. Executive Report — You receive a written report with prioritized findings and practical recommendations.

No tools are installed. No scanning or testing is performed. No implementation is required.

What This Review Covers

  • High-level cyber and data-handling risk
  • Access and permission exposure
  • Third-party and vendor dependency risk
  • Privacy and data-flow considerations
  • Organizational and operational risk signals
  • Areas where assumptions replace visibility

The focus is on decision clarity, not technical depth for its own sake.

What This Review Does Not Include

To set expectations clearly, this advisory does not include:

  • Penetration testing or vulnerability scanning
  • Compliance audits or certifications (SOC 2, ISO, HIPAA, etc.)
  • Security tooling selection or implementation
  • Incident response or forensic analysis
  • Ongoing security operations

This service is designed to inform leadership decisions, not replace security teams or vendors.

Who This Is For

  • Leadership teams seeking clarity before investing in security tooling
  • Organizations without dedicated internal security leadership
  • Companies navigating growth, change, or increased regulatory exposure
  • Teams who want an independent second opinion without sales pressure

Who This Is Not For

  • Organizations seeking compliance certification
  • Teams looking for penetration testing or remediation
  • Companies expecting managed security services
  • Environments requiring on-site assessment

Delivery

  • Fully remote and asynchronous
  • Delivered within 5–7 business days
  • Written, executive-ready report
  • Optional follow-up discussion available

Pricing

Essential Advisory — $1,500

Best for small to mid-sized organizations. Single environment or business unit, core cyber and privacy risk review, access and vendor dependency assessment, prioritized findings and recommendations, written executive report.

Comprehensive Advisory — $3,000 (Recommended)

Best for growing or business-critical environments. Multiple platforms or vendors, deeper access and data-flow analysis, expanded third-party risk review, business-impact prioritization, detailed executive report.

Complex / Multi-Environment Advisory — Starting at $5,000

For organizations with multiple teams or subsidiaries, complex vendor ecosystems, regulated or high-risk data exposure. Scope confirmed before engagement.

Optional Add-On: Executive Review Call — $300 — A 60-minute call to walk through findings and answer questions. Optional. No upsell pressure.

Every Strategic Cyber Risk Advisory delivers clear, written findings prioritized by risk and business impact — not by technical scan output or compliance checklists.
