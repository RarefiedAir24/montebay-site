LLM Automation for Ops Workflows

Operational teams spend too much time interpreting noise — tickets, logs, alerts, reports, and manual reviews — instead of resolving real issues.

Montebay designs LLM-augmented operational workflows that reduce manual effort, improve signal quality, and accelerate decision-making.

We apply large language models selectively, automating the text-heavy and decision-dense parts of operations while keeping humans in control.

What This Enables

  • Automated triage and classification of tickets, incidents, and operational requests
  • Clear summaries generated from logs, alerts, and incident timelines
  • Faster handoffs between teams with consistent, structured context
  • Reduced operational toil without introducing brittle automation

Where It's Used

  • Incident response and post-incident reporting
  • Support and operations queue management
  • Internal documentation and SOP generation
  • Executive-level operational summaries
