Who We Are

Solving Complex Problems with Clear Systems

Montebay Innovations is a consulting and technology studio focused on solving complex problems with clear, practical systems.

Founded by a senior systems and cloud architect, Montebay brings hands-on experience across cloud infrastructure, automation, product operations, and applied AI. The work is grounded in execution — not theory — shaped by years of building, operating, and fixing systems that businesses depend on every day.

How We Work

Clients come to Montebay when technology feels fragmented, overly complex, or unreliable. The role is to step in, bring clarity, and help design a path forward — whether that means advisory guidance, hands-on implementation, or both.

Montebay operates differently from traditional agencies or consulting firms. Engagements are focused, outcome-driven, and built around trust. The goal is not to sell tools or create dependency, but to help organizations make better decisions and build systems that quietly do their job.

At its core, Montebay Innovations exists to:

🎯

Reduce Complexity

Simplify systems and processes to make technology work for you, not against you.

⚙️

Improve Reliability

Build systems that operate consistently and dependably, day after day.

⏱️

Save Time & Mental Bandwidth

Free up your team to focus on what matters most by automating the routine.

🚀

Turn Strategy into Working Systems

Transform ideas and plans into real, functioning solutions that deliver results.

Whether advising on cloud architecture, streamlining operations through automation, or helping shape a new product from idea to execution, Montebay focuses on practical solutions that last.

Our Services

Strategic Consulting & Technical Guidance

Montebay Innovations provides strategic consulting and senior-level technical guidance for organizations dealing with complex systems, infrastructure decisions, and operational challenges.

The focus is clarity over complexity, outcomes over activity, and long-term stability over short-term fixes.

💡

Strategic Consulting & Advisory

Clear thinking before any build work begins.

Montebay works directly with founders, operators, and technical leaders to help them make confident decisions about technology, systems, and operations. Engagements focus on understanding what exists today, what's creating friction, and what actually needs to change.

This service is designed for organizations that need experienced judgment — not additional developers.

Typical consulting engagements include:

  • Technology and systems strategy
  • Architecture and platform decision support
  • Risk, reliability, and scalability reviews
  • Technical guidance for leadership teams
  • Independent second opinions on major initiatives
☁️

Cloud Architecture & Infrastructure (AWS)

Reliable foundations without over-engineering.

Montebay advises on, reviews, and improves AWS environments with an emphasis on security, reliability, and cost awareness. Work is centered on architecture, configuration, and operational posture — not ongoing infrastructure ownership.

Services include:

  • AWS architecture reviews and recommendations
  • Security and access posture assessments
  • Cost optimization strategy and analysis
  • Infrastructure simplification and cleanup
  • Scalability and resilience planning

Note: Montebay does not operate as a managed services provider or DevOps team.

⚙️

Operational Clarity & Targeted Automation

Reduce friction without creating new systems to maintain.

Montebay helps organizations simplify operations by identifying inefficiencies and designing practical improvements. Automation is applied selectively, only where it meaningfully reduces manual effort or risk.

This work explicitly avoids heavy custom pipeline development or systems that require ongoing babysitting.

Examples include:

  • Identifying and removing operational bottlenecks
  • Simplifying handoffs, approvals, and workflows
  • Improving visibility into systems and processes
  • Designing lightweight guardrails to prevent errors
  • Replacing brittle processes with durable solutions

Note: If a problem requires large-scale custom pipelines or continuous tuning, Montebay will recommend alternatives or decline the work.

🔧

System Review & Execution Support

Hands-on — when it matters.

When advisory alone isn't enough, Montebay provides targeted execution support focused on unblocking progress or stabilizing systems. This is short-term, senior-level involvement — not long-term delivery ownership.

Engagements may include:

  • Critical system fixes or remediation
  • Supporting internal teams during transitions
  • Short-term embedded advisory support
  • High-impact changes that require experienced oversight

The emphasis is on resolving the problem, not expanding scope.

🤝

Ongoing Advisory (Retainer)

A trusted technical perspective — without adding headcount.

For teams that need continuity, Montebay offers ongoing advisory relationships. These provide leadership teams with a consistent, senior technical partner to help assess risk, validate decisions, and evolve systems intentionally.

Well-suited for:

  • Growing teams without senior technical leadership
  • Founders navigating scale or complexity
  • Organizations managing critical or legacy systems
