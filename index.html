Who We Are

Solving Complex Problems with Clear Systems

Montebay Innovations is a consulting and technology studio focused on solving complex problems with clear, practical systems.

Founded by a senior systems and cloud architect, Montebay brings hands-on experience across cloud infrastructure, automation, product operations, and applied AI. The work is grounded in execution — not theory — shaped by years of building, operating, and fixing systems that businesses depend on every day.

How We Work

Clients come to Montebay when technology feels fragmented, overly complex, or unreliable. The role is to step in, bring clarity, and help design a path forward — whether that means advisory guidance, hands-on implementation, or both.

Montebay operates differently from traditional agencies or consulting firms. Engagements are focused, outcome-driven, and built around trust. The goal is not to sell tools or create dependency, but to help organizations make better decisions and build systems that quietly do their job.

At its core, Montebay Innovations exists to:

🎯

Reduce Complexity

Simplify systems and processes to make technology work for you, not against you.

⚙️

Improve Reliability

Build systems that operate consistently and dependably, day after day.

⏱️

Save Time & Mental Bandwidth

Free up your team to focus on what matters most by automating the routine.

🚀

Turn Strategy into Working Systems

Transform ideas and plans into real, functioning solutions that deliver results.

Whether advising on cloud architecture, streamlining operations through automation, or helping shape a new product from idea to execution, Montebay focuses on practical solutions that last.

What the Findings Look Like

Every Silent AWS Audit delivers clear, written findings prioritized by risk and impact — not by raw issue count or automated scan output.

The examples below are representative of the insights included in a typical audit. All findings are fully anonymized, written in plain language, and focused on practical decisions leadership teams can act on.

No dashboards. No noise. Just clarity.

Overly Permissive IAM Roles
High

Summary
Several IAM roles allow broader access than required, increasing blast radius without providing operational benefit.

Recommendation
Scope permissions to actual usage and separate administrative access from application roles to reduce risk without slowing teams down.

Underutilized Compute Resources
Medium

Summary
Multiple compute resources consistently operate well below expected utilization, contributing to unnecessary monthly spend.

Recommendation
Rightsize or consolidate affected resources to reduce baseline cost while maintaining current performance.

Single Points of Failure in Critical Paths
Medium

Summary
Key components rely on single resources without redundancy, increasing outage risk during failures or maintenance events.

Recommendation
Introduce minimal redundancy in critical paths to improve resilience without adding architectural complexity.

Accumulated Architectural Complexity
Low–Medium

Summary
Legacy components remain from earlier iterations, increasing cognitive overhead and operational friction.

Recommendation
Retire unused components and document the current-state architecture to simplify ongoing maintenance and decision-making.

This is the fastest way to get an expert assessment of your AWS environment before deciding on next steps.

Operational Clarity & Targeted Automation

Reduce friction without creating new systems to maintain.

Montebay helps organizations simplify operations by identifying inefficiencies and designing practical improvements. Automation is applied selectively, only where it meaningfully reduces manual effort or risk.

This work explicitly avoids heavy custom pipeline development or systems that require ongoing babysitting.

Examples include:

  • Identifying and removing operational bottlenecks
  • Simplifying handoffs, approvals, and workflows
  • Improving visibility into systems and processes
  • Designing lightweight guardrails to prevent errors
  • Replacing brittle processes with durable solutions

Note: If a problem requires large-scale custom pipelines or continuous tuning, Montebay will recommend alternatives or decline the work.
